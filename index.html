Plug it in with USB, or connect over Bluetooth.
  1. Touch the board: the face and eye colors swap (touch again to swap back), or the eyes follow your finger. Pick which under Touch & moves.
  2. Pick colors below: the board changes as you drag and remembers them, even after it's switched off.
  3. Save the ones you like to switch back with one click.

This app only talks to RoboEyes boards (Bluetooth name RoboEyes-XXXX). An EyeBuddy board uses the doomscroll-eyes app. Use desktop Chrome or Edge: they can talk to USB and Bluetooth.
